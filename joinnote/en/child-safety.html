Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Join Note (揪筆記, the "Service") is committed to a safe environment for everyone, especially minors. This page sets out our standards against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), how we comply with the law, and how to report concerns.

This English version is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Traditional Chinese version, the Traditional Chinese version prevails.

1Our commitment

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material and exploitation (CSAM/CSAE). No user may create, upload, store, share or distribute child sexual abuse material of any kind, or use the Service (including note sharing and co-editing) to groom, harass or exploit minors.

When we find a violation, we immediately remove the content, suspend or terminate the account, and report it to the relevant authorities as required by law.

2Safeguards

  • Notes are private by default; others can see a note only when its owner shares it with friends or study groups, and the owner chooses whether they can only view or also edit
  • Note owners can stop sharing at any time
  • The Service offers no public browsing of other people's notes and no messaging with strangers
  • Join Note has an in-app "Report this note" option for notes shared with you
  • Read or Not (same account) provides in-app reporting of users and content
  • Violating accounts are warned, restricted, suspended or permanently terminated depending on severity; because the account is shared, this also applies in Read or Not
  • A designated contact handles child safety reports, and we review and update our content policies regularly

3Legal compliance and reporting to authorities

We comply with all applicable child safety laws. When we find content or behavior involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, we report it to:

  • Taiwan: Criminal Investigation Bureau, National Police Agency; Ministry of Health and Welfare (113 protection hotline)
  • United States: National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC CyberTipline)
  • Other local or national law enforcement where the user is located

4How to report

You can report child safety concerns by:

  • Emailing our child safety contact (below), with the note title, the account's display name or screenshots if you can
  • Using the Report feature on a user or content in the Read or Not app

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police right away (in Taiwan, call the 113 hotline) — don't rely only on reporting to us.
