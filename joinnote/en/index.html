Features

Writes like paper.
Finds better than paper.

From quick lecture notes and annotated PDF handouts to exam review — all in one place.

✍️

Quick ink

No title, no template. An endless canvas or real notebook pages, with pen, highlighter, lasso and shape snapping.

🔍

Search your handwriting

Your handwriting is recognized on your device and search shows the exact line you wrote. Works offline.

✨

Constellation

Every note is a star. Join AI gathers related notes into galaxies, so you can see what you studied this term at a glance.

📄

PDFs, images and audio

Import handouts and write right on them. Record while you write, then tap any stroke to hear that moment again.

👥

Write together

Share a note with your Read or Not study group and write on the same page at once — see each other's ink and avatars.

📶

Works offline

Keep writing on the subway or in the library basement; everything syncs when you're back online. Folders, tags and bookmarks keep it tidy.

With Read or Not

One account.
Studying and notes, connected.

Join Note and Read or Not share an account. Start a timer in Read or Not and Join Note knows you're studying.

Timer sync

While a Read or Not timer runs, Join Note shows the subject and time at the top, and notes you write during the session are linked to that study record.
