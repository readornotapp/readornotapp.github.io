Handwritten notes · from the Read or Not team

Tap and start writing.
Name it later.

Join Note is a handwritten notebook built for studying. Open it and write. Your handwriting is searchable, your notes arrange themselves into a constellation, and your study group can write in the same note with you.

Writes like paper.
Finds better than paper.

From quick lecture notes and annotated PDF handouts to exam review — all in one place.

Feels like paper

Highlighters, shape snapping, real notebook paper

No toolbar while you write, just the Ink Orb. Tap to bloom pens, eraser, lasso and colors; double-tap for the eraser; drag it anywhere.

Search your handwriting

Recognized on your device, down to the line

Your handwriting is recognized on your device and search shows the exact line you wrote. Works offline.

Notes become a constellation

Join AI gathers related notes into galaxies

Every note is a star. Join AI gathers related notes into galaxies, so you can see what you studied this term at a glance.

Write together

See your study group's ink as it's drawn

Share a note with your Read or Not study group and write on the same page at once — see each other's ink and avatars.

And the rest

Quick ink

No title, no template. An endless canvas or real notebook pages, with pen, highlighter, lasso and shape snapping.

PDFs, images and audio

Import handouts and write right on them. Record while you write, then tap any stroke to hear that moment again.

Five kinds of paper

Blank, grid, lined, dotted or Cornell. Or scan paper pages with the camera and write right on them.

Transcripts

Turn a recording into a transcript in Chinese, English, Japanese or Korean. It's made on your device; the recording isn't sent to a transcription service.

Recall cards

Lasso part of a note and Join AI writes the questions. Review a few each day in Recall. Two minutes is enough.

Works offline

Keep writing on the subway or in the library basement; everything syncs when you're back online. Folders, tags and bookmarks keep it tidy.

With Read or Not

One account.
Studying and notes, connected.

Join Note and Read or Not share an account. Start a timer in Read or Not and Join Note knows you're studying.

Timer sync

While a Read or Not timer runs, Join Note shows the subject and time at the top, and notes you write during the session are linked to that study record.
