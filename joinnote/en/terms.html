1The Service

Join Note is a handwritten notes app. Its features include, but are not limited to:

  • Handwritten, typed, image, PDF and audio notes
  • Folders, tags, bookmarks and handwriting search
  • The constellation: notes organized into galaxies
  • Join AI organizing, recall-card questions, star icons and expression solving
  • Sharing and co-editing notes with study group members
  • Study timer and history sync with Read or Not
  • Offline use and automatic sync
  • Tables (including recognition from a photo), text styles and typing areas
  • Version history and restore
  • Flashcards and the Home Screen widget
  • Sharing with friends or study groups, as editors or viewers

4Sharing and co-editing

You can share a note with individual friends or study groups and choose whether each can edit or only view. Editors can change the note together with you; viewers can only read it. While editing together, other members can see your live ink, your online status and the page you're on.

Other members may change or delete content in a shared note. Share only what you're happy for them to see and edit, and keep your own copies of anything important. You can stop sharing at any time.

Editors can restore an earlier version from the version history, which affects everyone with access to the note; the content before the restore is saved as a version first and can be brought back.
